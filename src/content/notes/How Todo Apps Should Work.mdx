Let's consider a normal day in our lives.

## Different modes:

- just putting stuff into a list
  - Example: grocery shopping

- todo entry without thinking; just for writing it down
- entry with tags/context of usage
- todo entry with dependency tree (often a linearized list?)

- prioritization of todos
- placing todos in timeslots (aka. scheduling) (Usage examples: 'daily list', 'calendar')

- working on the highest prio todo (urgency, importance, ...) overall
- working on the best todo in 'project' (that should be tags)

- working on todo in timeslot 'now' (aka. calendar)

- getting an overview and saying no to things
  - Example: Kanban board
