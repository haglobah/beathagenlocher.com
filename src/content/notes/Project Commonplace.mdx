Over the last week, I've started working on something during the [Surplus](https://surplus.dev) startup accelerator program.

My week started with [Theo Ryzhenkov](https://theor.net) and me brainstorming together about what we might want to build, and then both of us naming a pretty similar idea/problem space.

## The Problem

The problem revolves around two situations:

1. I'm regularly reading a nice piece, or watching a good video, and think that I'd very much like to recommend it to friends of mine. But I'm currently watching the video, and doing that _right now_ makes me get out of that, so—_I don't_.
2. At different times, I'm regularly in the mood to read, watch, or listen to something, and _know_ that someone already recommended something to me—but I neither know who of my friends it was, nor in which messenger/email message/personal conversation I got the recommendation.

Both of these situations suck: That they exist means that we spend _more_ time on the platforms, just looking at what our feeds hand us.

## What We Built

And over the last few days, we made a thing: The name is _commonplace_ (after [commonplace books](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commonplace_book)) for now.
